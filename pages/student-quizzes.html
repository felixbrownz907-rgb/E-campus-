📋 Locked Exam Rules

  • You must have an approved exam registration for this exact course before an exam becomes available.
  • Supplementary and Deferred exams can only be written during your automatic 3-day window, 5 days after your Exam Period ends.
  • Avoid leaving this page or switching to another app once the exam begins. You will get a warning the first 3 times you do this.
  • On the 4th time you leave the page, your exam will be locked immediately and flagged for your lecturer's review.
  • Copying and pasting is disabled during the exam.
  • All students share the same exam end time — joining late means less time, not extra time.
  • Your answers are saved automatically as you go — if time runs out, you are marked on whatever you completed so far.
  • A locked exam can only be reopened by your lecturer. You will get a completely fresh attempt with full time — your previous answers will not be saved.
